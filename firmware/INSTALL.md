# Installing Alpaca on a Polyend Tracker Mini

1. Copy `TrackerMini_CE_1.0.0_NDLR10.ptf` (in this folder) to the `/Firmware` folder on the Mini's SD card.
2. On the Mini, go to Config > Firmware update and choose the file.
3. Once it has restarted, hold Menu, highlight NDLR Panel in the bottom-right tile and release Menu. It is also under Config > Info > NDLR Panel. Hold Menu again to go to another screen.

Controls: dpad moves the highlight, Middle presses, +1/-1/+10/-10 turn the highlighted encoder, Play = ALL, Shift = NDLR Shift (hold, or highlight SHIFT and press Middle to latch).

To record, press Down from the bottom-right dial to reach REC and press Middle. Press Middle again to stop. The take is saved as `/Recordings/NDLR0001.wav`, or the next free number.

To jam over a Tracker pattern, start the pattern first, then open the NDLR Panel. The panel follows the pattern's tempo and shows TRK.

Alpaca is a complete firmware, so installing it replaces what is on the Mini now. Tracker CE's recovery system and Polyend's own firmware are the way back.
